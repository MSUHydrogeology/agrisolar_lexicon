# Sources

**Verification status as of July 2026.** Entries under *Verified* were checked against publisher records or indexed metadata. Entries under *Unverified* are real sources whose full citation has not been confirmed — they are recorded here so the claim can be traced, but they should not appear in a proposal or publication until checked.

### Verified

**Ecovoltaics**
- Sturchio, M.A. & Knapp, A.K. (2023). Ecovoltaic principles for a more sustainable, ecologically informed solar energy future. *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 7:1746–1749. — *definitional source for "ecovoltaics."*
- Knapp, A.K. & Sturchio, M.A. (2024). Ecovoltaics in an increasingly water-limited world: an ecological perspective. *One Earth* 7(10):1705–1712. DOI 10.1016/j.oneear.2024.09.003. — *source for the operational/tracking-schedule argument.*
- Tölgyesi, C., Bátori, Z., Pascarella, J., Erdős, L., Török, P., Batáry, P., et al. (2023). Ecovoltaics: framework and future research directions to reconcile land-based solar power development with ecosystem conservation. *Biological Conservation* 285:110242. — *five-pillar framework; few-large vs. many-small.*
- Walston, L.J., et al. (2025). Ecovoltaic solar energy development can promote grassland bird communities. *Journal of Applied Ecology*. DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.70208.

**Agrivoltaic typology**
- Toledo, C. & Scognamiglio, A. (2021). Agrivoltaic systems design and assessment: a critical review, and a descriptive model towards a sustainable landscape vision (three-dimensional agrivoltaic patterns). *Sustainability* 13(12):6871. DOI 10.3390/su13126871. — *source for pore space and 3D pattern.*

**Solar grazing and vegetation management**
- McCall, J., Macdonald, J., Burton, R. & Macknick, J. (2023). Vegetation management cost and maintenance implications of different ground covers at utility-scale solar sites. *Sustainability* 15(7):5895. Also NREL/TP fy23osti/85418. — *source for §1.3 cost figures and the establishment-phase finding. Cite directly; see caution in §1.3.*
- Stewart, W.C., Scasta, J.D., Maierle, C., Ates, S., Burke, J.M. & Campbell, B.J. (2025). Vegetation management utilizing sheep grazing within utility-scale solar: agro-ecological insights and existing knowledge gaps in the United States. *Small Ruminant Research* 243:107439. DOI 10.1016/j.smallrumres.2025.107439.
- Andrew, A.C., Antoszewski, K., Goldberg, Z.A., Barter, J., Hain, L., DeSario, A., White, A., Roszell, C., Cole, E., Peterson, S. & Meys, B. (2025). Sheep grazing as sustainable vegetation management for solar energy production in the northeastern USA. *Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems* 9. DOI 10.3389/fsufs.2025.1625483. — *authorship spans ASGA, American Farmland Trust, and NYSERDA; useful for §2 positioning.*
- Bacon, et al. (2025). Agrivoltaic grazing systems for a sustainable future: a multi-disciplinary review and gap analysis. *Earth's Future*. DOI 10.1029/2024EF005429.

**Prairie strips**
- Luther, Z.R., Swinton, S.M. & Van Deynze, B. (2022). Potential supply of Midwest cropland for conversion to in-field prairie strips. *Land Economics* 98(2):274–291. DOI 10.3368/le.98.2.082020-0129R1. — *KBS LTER publication.*

- **Critical review of agrivoltaic typologies**, *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews* (2025). Article ID S1364032125011554; authors unconfirmed. — *source for elevated / vertical bifacial / tracking / semitransparent typology.*
- **Meta-analysis of climatic and design tipping points**, *Agronomy for Sustainable Development* (2025). DOI 10.1007/s13593-025-01060-z; authors unconfirmed. — *source for shade-tolerance classes.*
- **Comparative NSW/England large-scale solar regulatory analysis**, *Journal of Energy & Natural Resources Law* (2025). DOI 10.1080/02646811.2025.2540700; authors unconfirmed.
- **SEIA and NREL utility-scale thresholds.** Currently sourced from secondary commentary. Cite SEIA and NREL primary documents directly.
- **University of Illinois solar grazier survey (2023).** Known via trade coverage; primary report not located.
- **Kochendoerfer, N., Hain, A. & Thonney, M. (2019)**, Cornell Atkinson Center, on co-locating utility-scale solar with grazing sheep — cited but not independently checked.
- **Center for Rural Affairs**, *Opportunities for Solar Energy on Marginal Agricultural Lands*; **MSU Extension** prairie strip partial budget tool. Both located; full citations not yet formalized.
- **Land sharing / land sparing.** The framing in §2 is borrowed and currently uncited. Canonical anchors are generally taken to be Green et al. (2005) and Phalan et al. (2011), both in *Science* — neither verified here. **This debt should be paid explicitly before circulation.**
- **New Urbanism.** *The Lexicon of New Urbanism* (DPZ); SmartCode v9.2 and v10; Center for Applied Transect Studies; Form-Based Codes Institute; CNU. Form-based code rigidity critiques (Miami 21, Poundbury) are drawn from commentary rather than primary evaluation literature and should be strengthened.

*A note on method, since this document argues for shared language: the first draft of this list contained two citation errors, both introduced by supplying author names from memory where a source gave only a title. Both were caught by checking. The habit is worth keeping.*

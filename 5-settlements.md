# 5. Settlements

The templates, restated as resolutions of competing pressures at a scale zone and sharing position. Following New Urbanism's Archetype → Prototype → Type → Stereotype progression, these are **Types**: real, transformable instances, not an exhaustive set.

**Overstory Agrivoltaic** *(vineyard, orchard)*
[A2]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }}), occasionally [A3]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }}) · **[S1]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})** · *[structure-led]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})*
*Dominant pressures:* [agricultural continuity]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }}), [social license]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }}).
*Yielded:* [capital discipline]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }}) (high clearance is expensive), [generation yield]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }}) (row spacing serves the crop).
*Signature levers:* high clearance, possibly semitransparent modules, row geometry set by crop light requirements.

**Grazed Habitat Array**
[A4]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }})–[A5]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }}) · **[S2]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})–[S3]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})** · *[management-led]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})*
*Dominant pressures:* generation yield, [operational simplicity]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }}) — grazing approaches cost parity with mowing rather than beating it outright, its vegetation-management advantage back-loaded to the mature-project years once the establishment invoices are behind, with restorative benefit following.
*Yielded:* agricultural continuity in the strong sense (grazing is a service contract, not the farm's own operation).
*Signature levers:* contract-enterprise management regime, rotation logistics, fence-gate topology, water access.

**Buffer-Integrated Array** *(prairie strips)*
A3 · **S3** · *[siting-led]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})*
*Dominant pressures:* [restorative ambition]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }}) with a specific service target (erosion, nutrient runoff), social license.
*Yielded:* some generation yield to buffer geometry.
*Signature levers:* placement logic along hydrologic features, ground-layer composition, conservation payment stacking.

**Marginal-Land Diversification Array**
A2–A3 · **[S4]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})** · *siting-led*
*Dominant pressures:* farm economic resilience, capital discipline.
*Yielded:* generation yield (marginal ground is rarely optimally shaped or oriented).
*Signature levers:* placement logic within field, ground layer determined by what marginal ground supports.

> **Note the kinship.** This settlement and the buffer-integrated one share an economic engine: prairie strips pay because they cost less than farming consistently low-yielding ground, which is the same partial-budget logic driving marginal-land solar siting. They differ mainly in whether the spared ground carries a designed ecological function or an energy function. Both are siting-led, and both depend on the A2–A3 latitude that makes choosing the ground possible at all — which is precisely why neither scales upward without changing into something else. Two settlements, one insight, and a good illustration of what the pressure framing reveals that a template catalog hides.

### Associated Terminology

Defined here (this section is their collection point):

**Settlement** — A way the [design pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#3-design-pressures' | relative_url }}) have been resolved at a particular scale zone and sharing position; the four templates above are settlements, not the object of the lexicon.

**Type** — Following New Urbanism's Archetype → Prototype → Type → Stereotype progression, the settlements are Types: real, transformable instances, not an exhaustive set.

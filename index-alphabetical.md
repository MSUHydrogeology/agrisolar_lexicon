# Index — Alphabetical

Every defined or coined term in the lexicon, in one place. The built-in
site search (top of every page) complements this index: search finds a word
anywhere in the prose, while this list is the curated set of terms the
document actually defines, each pointing to where it is introduced.

Zone codes **A1–A5** are grouped under **A**; sharing positions **S1–S4**
under **S**.

---

## A

**A1 — Farmstead** — <100 kW, behind-the-meter, single owner-operator, existing farm labour — [1.2 The Zones]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }})

**A2 — Field** — 100 kW–1 MW, behind-meter to distribution, landowner with possibly one lease counterparty — [1.2 The Zones]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }})

**A3 — Parcel** — 1–10 MW, distribution / community solar, multi-party, purpose-built ground management — [1.2 The Zones]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }})

**A4 — Landscape** — 10–50 MW, developer-led institutional capital, contract-enterprise management — [1.2 The Zones]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }})

**A5 — Regional** — 50 MW+, transmission, utility/IPP, contract enterprise at ranch scale — [1.2 The Zones]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#12-the-zones' | relative_url }})

**Agricultural continuity** — design pressure: keep working land working — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

**Agrisolar** — agroenergy specific to photovoltaics; this lexicon's primary term of art, neutral across the sharing–sparing axis — [Agrisolar]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#agrisolar' | relative_url }})

**Agrivoltaics** — crop or livestock production within the array footprint (S1–S2); *cf.* agrophotovoltaics, photovoltaic agriculture — [Agrivoltaics]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#agrivoltaics' | relative_url }})

**Agroenergy** — energy infrastructure designed to interact intentionally with agricultural or ecological land function; umbrella term — [Agroenergy]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#agroenergy' | relative_url }})

## C

**Capital discipline** — design pressure: minimize installed cost per watt — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

**Carrying capacity** — season-long forage the land sustains over time; a grazing design variable — [1.3 Why the transect earns its place]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#13-why-the-transect-earns-its-place' | relative_url }})

**Co-location** — panels and a land use share a parcel without functional interdependence; *cf.* integration — [Co-location vs. Integration]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#co-location-vs-integration' | relative_url }})

**Contract grazing** — grazing delivered as a contracted vegetation-management service by a party who does not live on site — [1.3 Why the transect earns its place]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#13-why-the-transect-earns-its-place' | relative_url }})

**Covarying attribute bundle** — the attributes that move together along the transect, so naming a zone tells you many things at once — [1.1 Why scale, and why a transect rather than bins]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#11-why-scale-and-why-a-transect-rather-than-bins' | relative_url }})

## D

**Design levers** — the degrees of freedom — structural, land-and-ecology, institutional — through which pressures get settled — [3.3 Design levers]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#33-design-levers' | relative_url }})

**Design pressures** — the competing forces every site must settle; unlike axes they have direction, conflict, and advocates — [3. Design Pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/' | relative_url }})

## E

**Ecovoltaics** — solar development incorporating ecological understanding into array design and management, co-prioritizing ecosystem function with generation (typically S3) — [Ecovoltaics]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#ecovoltaics' | relative_url }})

## F

**Feasible wedge** — the feasible region across scale and sharing: all four sharing positions stay open through A3, narrowing toward A5 — [2.2 The feasible region is a wedge, not a rectangle]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#22-the-feasible-region-is-a-wedge-not-a-rectangle' | relative_url }})

**Footprint** — land physically occupied by racking, panels, and access infrastructure; paired with influence zone — [Footprint vs. Influence Zone]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#footprint-vs-influence-zone' | relative_url }})

## G

**Generation yield** — design pressure: maximize energy per unit land — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

**Ground Layer** — whatever occupies the surface beneath and between panels; primary carrier of restorative function — [Ground Layer]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#ground-layer' | relative_url }})

## I

**Influence Zone** — the larger, typically asymmetric area whose hydrology, microclimate, soil, or wildlife movement is measurably affected — [Footprint vs. Influence Zone]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#footprint-vs-influence-zone' | relative_url }})

**Integration** — functional interdependence — panel height set by livestock clearance, spacing by forage light, tracking by photosynthetic timing — [Co-location vs. Integration]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#co-location-vs-integration' | relative_url }})

## L

**Land Equivalent Ratio (LER)** — standard dual-use productivity metric; handles S3 ground layers poorly — [Land Equivalent Ratio (LER)]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#land-equivalent-ratio-ler' | relative_url }})

## M

**Management-led** — restoration mode: placement is a given; ground cover, grazing regime, and operational schedule carry everything — [1.4 Siting latitude and the restoration mode shift]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})

## O

**Offtake conformity** — design pressure: satisfy the criteria of whoever buys the power, attributes, or tax position — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

**Operational simplicity** — design pressure: minimize management burden across a 25–40 year asset life — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

## P

**Pore Space** — the three-dimensional volume between modules and between modules and ground; frames clearance as habitat and working space — [Pore Space]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#pore-space' | relative_url }})

## R

**Restoration mode** — whether restorative work is delivered by choice of ground, array geometry, or operation — Siting-led, Structure-led, or Management-led — [1.4 Siting latitude and the restoration mode shift]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})

**Restorative** — the frame of the whole lexicon: ground-layer function as a design objective co-equal with generation — [Restorative]({{ '/4-general-terminology/#restorative' | relative_url }})

**Restorative ambition** — design pressure: make ecological, hydrological, and economic resilience co-equal with generation — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

## S

**S1 — Full sharing** — production continues within the array footprint, in the same ground — [2.1 The positions]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})

**S2 — Functional sharing** — ground layer productive but subordinate to the array; grazing, forage — [2.1 The positions]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})

**S3 — Substituted function** — ground layer delivers ecological rather than agricultural function — [2.1 The positions]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})

**S4 — Sparing** — array occupies ground removed from production because it was low-yielding or already idle — [2.1 The positions]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/#21-the-positions' | relative_url }})

**Scale Transect** — a single gradient from farmstead to regional that most other terms are read against; defined by covarying bundles, not capacity — [1. The Scale Transect]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/' | relative_url }})

**Settlement** — a way the design pressures have been resolved at a particular scale zone and sharing position — [5. Settlements]({{ '/5-settlements/' | relative_url }})

**Sharing–Sparing Axis** — the scale-orthogonal dimension: whether the array shares land with production or spares low-value ground from it — [2. The Sharing–Sparing Axis]({{ '/2-the-sharingsparing-axis/' | relative_url }})

**Siting latitude** — the designer's freedom over where the array goes; governed by the operator at A1, by the land market and grid topology at A5 — [1.4 Siting latitude and the restoration mode shift]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})

**Siting-led** — restoration mode: the restorative work is done by the choice of ground; requires A2–A3 latitude — [1.4 Siting latitude and the restoration mode shift]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})

**Social license** — design pressure: secure and sustain community acceptance — [3.1 The pressures]({{ '/3-design-pressures/#31-the-pressures' | relative_url }})

**Stocking density** — instantaneous animals per unit area at a moment; diverges from carrying capacity — [1.3 Why the transect earns its place]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#13-why-the-transect-earns-its-place' | relative_url }})

**Structure-led** — restoration mode: array geometry is the instrument — clearance, row spacing, module transmissivity — [1.4 Siting latitude and the restoration mode shift]({{ '/1-the-scale-transect/#14-siting-latitude-and-the-restoration-mode-shift' | relative_url }})

## T

**Type** — following New Urbanism's Archetype → Prototype → Type → Stereotype progression, the settlements are Types: real, transformable instances — [5. Settlements]({{ '/5-settlements/' | relative_url }})
